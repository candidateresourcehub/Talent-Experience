Our approach

It's not just the outcome. It's how people experience getting there.

Every message, conversation, interview and update shapes how a candidate feels. We want an experience that is thoughtful and consistent without losing the personal connection that matters.

Human first

Make space for real people, individual circumstances and honest conversations. Treat candidates with respect at every stage.

Clear and considered

Set expectations, explain what happens next and communicate proactively, especially when plans or timelines change.

Always learning

Use feedback, observation and practical measures to understand what helps and where small changes could make a difference.

Our learning cycle

Listen, test, learn and refine.

Start with an idea, shape it into something practical, test it in the real process and pay attention to what happens. Then refine it and review what we've learned.

Why Talent Experience matters

A better candidate experience is good for people and good for business.

Talent Experience is not an extra layer around recruitment. It is part of how we deliver our service, build trust and represent Untapped. The way candidates experience each interaction shapes their confidence in us, their willingness to stay engaged and the impression they carry forward, whatever the outcome.

Builds trust in Untapped

Consistent, thoughtful interactions strengthen our reputation and help candidates feel confident in the people and business representing them.

Strengthens delivery

Clear expectations, timely communication and joined-up handovers help reduce uncertainty and avoid preventable friction across recruitment and placement.

Creates learning and insight

Candidate feedback and recruiter observations help us recognise what works, address recurring issues and make informed improvements.

The standard we are working towards: a consistent level of care, clarity and professionalism, delivered with a personal touch that reflects each candidate’s circumstances.

The phases show how the work connects, from the resources and touchpoints already created to the way each recruiter can put Talent Experience into practice with their own candidates.

Candidate feedback snapshot

Listen first. Look for patterns. Improve with care.

A snapshot of feedback from a small group of candidates. It gives us useful early signals about what is working and where we can make the experience better.

What we've learnt

Candidates value warm, personable recruiter interactions, clear and proactive communication, useful interview preparation and constructive feedback when an application is unsuccessful. Feedback on the overall experience has been positive, with candidates also indicating that they would recommend Untapped. The opportunity is to protect these strengths and make them consistent throughout the journey.

What we can do better

Waiting for client decisions can create uncertainty. When there is no final answer yet, candidates still value a short update, an honest explanation of where things stand and a realistic expectation of when they may hear more.

This reflects feedback from a small group and should be read as an early indication of themes, not as representative research. Feedback is considered in aggregate, with candidate privacy protected.

Phase 6

Insights & Reporting

Keep measurement useful and proportionate. Leading indicators include clarity, communication, preparation, support, resource engagement and process friction. Lagging indicators include overall feedback, withdrawals, offer declines and post-placement experience. Combine small meaningful measures with qualitative themes, and avoid drawing strong conclusions from isolated responses.

Shared ownership

One shared standard. Everyone plays a part.

Talent Experience works best when it is part of how we work every day. The framework gives us a shared direction, while each recruiter remains responsible for the relationship and experience of their own candidates.

Every recruiter · Own the relationship

Bring the principles into your candidate conversations and follow-ups. Gather feedback from your own candidates and notice what is helping or creating friction.

Talent Experience · Guide and connect

The person coordinating the function keeps the framework and resources relevant, shares reminders and useful examples, and helps connect feedback into themes and improvement opportunities.

The team · Share and improve

Share candidate feedback, positive outcomes and ideas with colleagues. Learning is a shared responsibility, not something that sits with one person.

How we keep learning: use the shared candidate feedback form, or your own appropriate tracking method, to capture feedback from your candidates. Bring useful themes, wins and suggestions to a weekly team discussion, or share them as they arise when something is worth passing on. The aim is to keep learning visible and make practical improvements together.

Putting it into practice

Make it your own. Keep the experience human.

The resources are a foundation, not a script or a one-size-fits-all process. Use the shared principles to guide your judgement, adapt your approach to the person, role and client in front of you, and contribute to a consistent standard of care across Untapped.

Notice

Pay attention to candidate questions, moments of uncertainty and what works particularly well.

Use your judgement

Adapt the timing and level of support to the individual. Keep communication clear, personal and honest.

Keep learning

Share useful observations, look for patterns and make small improvements part of your own recruiter practice.
